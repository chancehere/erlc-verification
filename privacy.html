Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Our ER:LC Discord bot provides Roblox account verification for members of our Discord server.

Information We Collect

Our bot may collect Discord user IDs, Roblox user IDs, Roblox usernames, and verification status to link accounts and manage verified roles.

How We Use Information

We use this information to verify Roblox accounts, link accounts, manage verification roles, and help prevent fraudulent verification.

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Roblox, Discord, and the hosting or verification services used to operate the bot.

Account Security

We never ask users to provide their Roblox passwords. Roblox sign-in is handled through the applicable verification service.

Data Removal

Users may contact our Discord server's administration team to request account unlinking or deletion of stored verification information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the administration team through our official Discord server.
